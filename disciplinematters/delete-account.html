Delete your Discipline Matters account

Deleting your account permanently removes your sign-in identity and all data associated with it — rituals, streaks, workouts, nutrition logs, recovery scores, and Ascendant progress. This cannot be undone.

Delete directly in the app (immediate)

  1. Open Discipline Matters on your device.
  2. Open the Morning tab and tap the settings icon in the header.
  3. Scroll to the Account section and choose Delete Account.
  4. Confirm. If asked, re-enter your password. Your account and all server data are deleted immediately, and all data stored on the device is wiped.

Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, email contact@finndigital.net from the address tied to your account with the subject "Discipline Matters account deletion". We verify ownership and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your authentication account (email or Google sign-in), any server-side entitlement records, and every locally stored record on your device. Active subscriptions are managed separately through Google Play subscriptions — deleting your account does not cancel a subscription.
