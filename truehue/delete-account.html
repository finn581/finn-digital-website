Delete your TrueHue account

This permanently removes your TrueHue account. It cannot be undone.

What gets deleted

  • Your TrueHue sign-in account and its email address
  • Your saved app preferences tied to that account

What is stored only on your device

TrueHue keeps your scans, palettes, projects and clients on your phone — they are never uploaded to our servers. Deleting your account here does not erase them. To remove that data, delete your account from inside the app (Settings → Delete account), or uninstall TrueHue.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a TrueHue Plus subscription. Cancel it in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions, or on the App Store under Subscriptions, depending on where you purchased.
