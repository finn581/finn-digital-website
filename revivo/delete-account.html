Delete your Revivo account

Revivo is operated by Finn Digital LLC. You can request account and associated-data deletion without installing the app.

Request deletion by email

Email a Revivo account deletion request to support@finndigital.net with the subject Revivo account deletion request.

Send the request from the email used to sign in, and tell us whether you use Android or iPhone. For Sign in with Apple, include your Apple relay email if available. For an anonymous account, include the account identifier if you have it. We will help verify ownership before deleting data. Never send passwords, verification codes or payment-card details.

What is deleted

The request covers your Revivo account, original and restored photos, saved versions, animation jobs and videos, People names and face representations, reference photos, albums and sharing records. We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days and will contact you if verification or a legal retention requirement affects the request.

Limited purchase-verification and deletion records may be retained to prevent duplicate credit claims and enforce deletion. These records do not contain your deleted photos. Copies exported or saved by other people cannot be recalled. Third-party processing and retention are described in the privacy policy.

Delete inside Revivo

Open Settings → Delete Account and confirm. If prompted, sign in again to verify ownership. Wait for the request confirmation before closing the app.

Cancel subscriptions separately

Deleting Revivo or your account does not cancel a store subscription. Android users can open Google Play subscriptions. On iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Revivo.
