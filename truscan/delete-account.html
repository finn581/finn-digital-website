Delete your TruScan account

TruScan is published by Finn Digital LLC. You can delete your TruScan account and the data it holds inside the app, or by e-mail without installing it. This page applies to TruScan on Android, iPhone and iPad.

Delete inside TruScan

Open Profile → Delete account (in the Account section) and confirm. It works for an anonymous account as well as a signed-in one. If TruScan asks, sign in again to prove it is you, then wait for the confirmation before you close the app.

Request deletion by e-mail

Write to contact@finndigital.net with the subject TruScan account deletion request, from the address you signed in with, and tell us whether you use Android or iPhone and iPad and which sign-in you used (Google or Apple). For an anonymous account, say so. We verify that the account is yours before deleting anything, and we aim to finish within 30 days. Never send passwords, verification codes or payment-card details.

What is deleted

Everything TruScan keeps for you in the cloud: your synced scans, their pages and thumbnails, the photographs you enhanced with AI and all earlier versions of them, the records that describe them, and the account itself. If you turned on Encrypt my library, the encrypted copies go too.

In TruScan for Android, if any of your cloud data can’t be deleted, your account is kept so you can try again.

In the iPhone and iPad app, if the app cannot read your cloud data it keeps your account so you can try again, but an item that will not delete is skipped and the account is still removed; tell us at contact@finndigital.net if you think something was left behind.

What stays

  • The scans on your phone or tablet are not touched. Delete them in the app, or remove the app.
  • A synced scan you delete in the app waits in Recently Deleted for 30 days, then its cloud copy is removed; choose Delete Now to remove it sooner.
  • Limited transaction, fraud-prevention, security, backup and legally required records may remain where necessary, as the Privacy Policy says. Copies you exported or shared stay with whoever received them.

Delete some data without deleting the account

You do not have to delete the account to remove data. When you delete a scan or photograph in TruScan, its copy in the cloud is deleted with it (a synced scan after its 30 days in Recently Deleted, or at once with Delete Now). Turning sync off stops future syncing but does not by itself delete what is already stored.

Your TruScan Pro purchase

On Android, TruScan Pro is a one-time purchase in Google Play, not a subscription: nothing renews and there is nothing to cancel. It belongs to your Google Play account, not to your TruScan account, so deleting the TruScan account does not remove it; Restore Purchases brings Pro back with the same Google Play account. Refunds follow Google Play’s refund policy.

On iPhone and iPad, deleting TruScan or your account does not cancel an App Store subscription. Open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → TruScan Pro. The lifetime purchase there is not a subscription, so there is nothing to cancel.
