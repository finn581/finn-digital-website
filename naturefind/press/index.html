About NatureFind

One hundred words, ready to republish as-is.

NatureFind puts every United States national park, state park, hiking trail and campground on one map — more than 470 National Park Service sites alongside state and local parks from OpenStreetMap and campgrounds from Recreation.gov. Its AI layer identifies any animal or plant from a photo, builds day-by-day trip itineraries, and briefs visitors on live weather, air quality and active wildfires before they leave the trailhead. A community feed collects wildlife sightings from fellow explorers, and a built-in GPS recorder tracks every hike. NatureFind is free on iOS and Android, made by independent studio Finn Digital LLC.

What makes it different

One map, every park type
National, state and local parks, trails and campgrounds in a single view with 13 toggleable layers — no incumbent app combines all of them.
AI wildlife & plant ID
Photograph any animal, bird, plant or tree; the app returns the species, confidence level, range, and safety notes.
Know before you go
Live weather, air quality and active wildfire perimeters on every park page — conditions surfaced before the drive, not at the trailhead.
Honest pricing
The free tier is the full map plus community and hike recording. Plus can be bought once, forever — no subscription required.
